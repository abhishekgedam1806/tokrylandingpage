Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 1, 2026

At TOKRY Lifestyle Pvt. Ltd. ("TOKRY", "we", "our", or "us"), we are deeply dedicated to honoring and protecting the personal privacy of our customers. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent practices regarding the collection, processing, protection, and disclosure of information when you interact with our website or purchase the TOKRY Diwali Festive Décor Set.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us on our website, we collect the necessary details required to deliver your package smoothly:

  • Contact Information: Full Name, Mobile / WhatsApp Phone Number, and Email Address.
  • Delivery Address: Flat/House Number, Building Name, Street, City, State, and 6-digit Pincode.
  • Order & Transaction Details: Selected package (1 Set for ₹999 (MRP ₹1,499) or 2 Sets for ₹1,799), chosen payment mode (Cash on Delivery or Online Payment), and generated Order ID.
  • Technical Information: Device type, browser type, IP address, and anonymized user activity to optimize page loading speed.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data exclusively for direct fulfillment and customer convenience:

  • To book and dispatch your insured parcel via accredited courier networks (Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC).
  • To send real-time order confirmation, airway bill tracking numbers, and delivery updates directly via SMS and WhatsApp.
  • To coordinate doorstep delivery, address verification, and collect COD payments safely.
  • To resolve customer service inquiries, handle transit damage claims, and provide replacements.

3. 100% Zero-Data Selling Guarantee

We never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal contact information to any third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or data brokers. Your data is used purely for your TOKRY purchases.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We only share necessary operational details with trusted partners who assist in our business execution under strict non-disclosure obligations:

  • Logistics & Courier Partners: Only your name, shipping address, and phone number are shared with Delhivery / BlueDart to physically transport the box to your doorstep.
  • Payment Gateways: For online transactions, all card details and UPI authentications are processed directly through RBI-compliant, PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways with 256-bit encryption. TOKRY does not store your CVV, debit/credit card numbers, or UPI PINs.

5. Data Security Measures

Our website employs industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL) encryption protocols. Internal access to customer orders is restricted to authorized fulfillment personnel on a need-to-know basis.

6. Your Rights

You have the complete right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to your delivery address before dispatch, or request the deletion of your contact records from our promotional notifications at any time by messaging our helpline.
