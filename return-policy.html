At TOKRY, customer satisfaction is our sacred commitment. Because our festive pieces are handcrafted with authentic MDF and premium finishes, we take supreme care in packing.

2. Return Eligibility & Window

If you wish to return the complete set for reasons other than damage (e.g. change of mind):

  1. Return requests must be initiated within 7 calendar days from the date of confirmed delivery.
  2. The festive box must be intact with all 6 components, including the 220V power cord, silken tassels, and laser foam packaging.
  3. Used, altered, or artificially soiled pieces cannot be accepted for return due to the sacred festive nature of the items.

3. Refund Processing & Timelines

Once the return package is collected and verified at our fulfillment hub:

  • Prepaid Orders (UPI, Cards, NetBanking): The full refund amount of ₹999 (or bundle value) will be credited directly back to the original source payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Our support team will request your preferred UPI ID (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or bank account details. The refund will be initiated instantly via IMPS within 24 to 48 hours of verification.

4. Order Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your order free of charge at any time prior to courier dispatch. Orders are typically processed and dispatched within 12 to 24 hours.

To cancel an order, simply message us on WhatsApp with your Order ID. If the shipment has already been picked up by our courier partner (Delhivery / BlueDart), you may refuse delivery at your doorstep, and any prepaid amount will be refunded upon package return.
